\newpage
\begin{center}
    {\Large \textbf{HYMN 312}}
    \\
    \textit{C.M. MARTYRDOM}
    \\
    \textit{\small [Moderate]}
\end{center}
\vspace{1em}

\begin{enumerate}
    \item O LORD, we earnestly entreat \\
        That in this solemn hour \\
        The bless\`ed Holy Paraclete \\
        May manifest His power.

    \item May He, like mighty rushing wind \\
        Upon the waves beneath, \\
        Move with one impulse every mind; \\
        One soul, one feeling breathe.

    \item May He the young, the old inspire \\
        With wisdom from above, \\
        And give us hearts and tongues of fire, \\
        To pray and praise and love.

    \item Spirit of light! May He explore \\
        And chase our gloom away, \\
        With lustre shining more and more \\
        Unto the perfect day.

    \item May He, the bless\`ed Paraclete, \\
        Who with us doth abide \\
        God's gracious purpose to complete, \\
        Be day by day our Guide. Amen.

\end{enumerate}

\newpage
\begin{center}
    {\Large \textbf{HYMN 313}}
    \\
    \textit{C.M. ABBEY}
    \\
    \textit{\small [Moderate]}
\end{center}
\vspace{1em}

\begin{enumerate}
    \item OH, what a comfort 'tis to know \\
        We have, whate'er betide, \\
        A mighty Helper here below, \\
        Who ever will abide.

    \item The Holy Spirit in His grace, \\
        With power that must prevail, \\
        Has deigned to make His dwelling place \\
        These mortal bodies frail.

    \item God's holy pledge sent down from heaven \\
        To seal us for His own; \\
        The bless\`ed earnest He has given \\
        Of joys that shall be known.

    \item Unerring Guide, He leads us in \\
        Truth's pathway by the word; \\
        And, if our spirits droop within, \\
        True comfort will afford.

    \item When we the Father's presence seek \\
        In child-like faltering prayer, \\
        He helps our supplications weak \\
        By intercessions there.

    \item May we His holy strivings know, \\
        And to His voice give heed; \\
        So by Him live and walk and grow, \\
        As sons of God indeed.

\end{enumerate}

\newpage
\begin{center}
    {\Large \textbf{HYMN 314}}
    \\
    \textit{8.6.8.4. ST. CUTHBERT}
    \\
    \textit{\small [Moderate]}
\end{center}
\vspace{1em}

\begin{enumerate}
    \item OUR blest Redeemer, ere He breathed \\
        His tender, last farewell, \\
        A Guide, a Comforter bequeathed, \\
        With us to dwell.

    \item He came in semblance of a dove, \\
        With sheltering wings outspread, \\
        The holy balm of peace and love \\
        On earth to shed.

    \item He came in tongues of living flame \\
        To teach, convince, subdue; \\
        All powerful as the wind He came, \\
        As viewless too.

    \item He came sweet influence to impart, \\
        A gracious, willing Guest, \\
        Where He can find one humble heart \\
        Wherein to rest.

    \item And His that gentle voice we hear, \\
        Soft as the breath of e'en, \\
        That chides each fault, that calms each fear, \\
        And speaks of heaven.

    \item And every virtue we possess, \\
        And every victory won, \\
        And every thought of holiness, \\
        Are His alone.

    \item Spirit of purity and grace, \\
        Our weakness pitying see; \\
        Oh, make our hearts Thy dwelling place, \\
        And worthier Thee. Amen.

\end{enumerate}

\newpage
\begin{center}
    {\Large \textbf{HYMN 315}}
    \\
    \textit{L.M. SAMSON}
    \\
    \textit{\small [Moderate]}
\end{center}
\vspace{1em}

\begin{enumerate}
    \item HAPPY am I! a gracious Friend \\
        To dwell with me doth condescend; \\
        Though oft my foolish thoughts may roam, \\
        This Friend still makes my heart His home.

    \item When I in Christ the Lord believed, \\
        And everlasting life received, \\
        He sent His Spirit from above, \\
        That I might understand His love.

    \item 'Twas He who made me know my sin, \\
        By nature wicked and unclean; \\
        'Twas He revealed the Son of God, \\
        And all the virtue of His blood.

    \item 'Tis He who teaches me to pray \\
        To God my Father every day; \\
        To find my pleasure in the Lord, \\
        And in His holy written word.

    \item When like a sheep I go astray \\
        And turn to mine own foolish way, \\
        He brings me back in love and grace, \\
        Again to seek my Father's face.

    \item He teaches me I'm very frail, \\
        But by His help I can prevail \\
        O'er Satan, sin, and every foe \\
        Whom I may meet while here below.

    \item For Christ, my Master's quick return, \\
        'Tis He who teaches me to yearn; \\
        The Paraclete, who, wondrous grace! \\
        Makes my poor heart His dwelling place.

\end{enumerate}

\newpage
\begin{center}
    {\Large \textbf{HYMN 316}}
    \\
    \textit{C.M. OLNEY}
    \\
    \textit{\small [Moderate]}
\end{center}
\vspace{1em}

\begin{enumerate}
    \item GRANT that Thy Holy Spirit, Lord, \\
        May let His beams arise, \\
        Dispel the darkness from our minds, \\
        And open all our eyes.

    \item Oh may He cheer our troubled hearts, \\
        That heavenly Paraclete; \\
        Give us to lie, in humble faith; \\
        At our Redeemer's feet.

    \item May He revive our drooping faith, \\
        Our doubts and fears remove; \\
        And kindle in our breasts the flame \\
        Of never-dying love.

    \item May He convince us of our guilt, \\
        And speak of Jesus' blood; \\
        And to our wondering view reveal \\
        The mighty love of God.

    \item May He show us the Son of Man, \\
        Who rules the courts of bliss, \\
        The Lord of hosts, the Mighty God, \\
        Th' eternal Prince of Peace.

    \item For, Lord, 'tis His to cleanse the heart, \\
        To sanctify the soul; \\
        By opening up Thy precious word, \\
        To new-create the whole.

    \item May He, who dwells in all our hearts, \\
        Our minds from sin set free, \\
        That we may know and praise and love \\
        One God in Persons Three.

\end{enumerate}
