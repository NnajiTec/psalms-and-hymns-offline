\newpage
\begin{center}
    {\Large \textbf{HYMN 89*\textdagger{}}}
    \\
    \textit{7.7.7.6. EVELYN}
\end{center}
\vspace{1em}

\begin{enumerate}
    \item FOR the bread and for the wine, \\
        For the pledge that seals Him mine, \\
        For the words of love divine, \\
        We give Thee thanks, O Lord.

    \item Only bread, and only wine, \\
        Yet to faith the solemn sign \\
        Of the heavenly and divine; \\
        We give Thee thanks, O Lord.

    \item For the words that turn the eye \\
        To the cross at Calvary, \\
        Bidding us in faith draw nigh, \\
        We give Thee thanks, O Lord.

    \item For the words that fragrance breathe, \\
        These plain symbols underneath, \\
        Words that His own peace bequeath, \\
        We give Thee thanks, O Lord.

    \item For the words that tell of home, \\
        Pointing us beyond the tomb, \\
        "Do ye this until I come;" \\
        We give Thee thanks, O Lord.

    \item Till He come we take the bread, \\
        Type of Him on whom we feed, \\
        Him who liveth and was dead; \\
        We give Thee thanks, O Lord.

    \item Till He come we drink the wine, \\
        Of His blood the solemn sign, \\
        Telling each of love divine; \\
        We give Thee thanks, O Lord.

    \item For that coming here foreshown, \\
        For that day to man unknown, \\
        For the glory and the throne, \\
        We give Thee thanks, O Lord.

\end{enumerate}

\newpage
\begin{center}
    {\Large \textbf{HYMN 90*\textdagger{}}}
    \\
    \textit{8.8.8.4. ALMSGIVING}
\end{center}
\vspace{1em}

\begin{enumerate}
    \item THIS broken bread and poured-out wine \\
        Sweet hallowed mem\`ories enshrine \\
        Of that great sacrifice divine--- \\
        Jesus, our Lord.

    \item Though loomed Golgotha in His view, \\
        His forethought made this statute new--- \\
        "This in remembrance of Me do;" \\
        Jesus, our Lord.

    \item The loaf He took spake of that frame, \\
        Prepared by God for Him who came \\
        To manifest His Father's name--- \\
        Jesus, our Lord.

    \item He gave God thanks, the loaf He brake; \\
        "My body this, given for your sake," \\
        Words such as these in love He spake, \\
        Jesus, our Lord.

    \item How solemn is the broken bread! \\
        Sign of His holy body, dead, \\
        Who suffered in our room and stead; \\
        Jesus, our Lord.

    \item The cup doth mem\`ories impart \\
        Of His kind sympathetic heart, \\
        For He of blood and flesh took part; \\
        Jesus, our Lord.

    \item The cup of wine poured out we see; \\
        This speaks of pardon full and free, \\
        But tells of His deep agony--- \\
        Jesus, our Lord.

    \item His woe and travail now are o'er; \\
        He who sin's dreadful burden bore \\
        Now lives triumphant evermore! \\
        Jesus, our Lord.

    \item A few times more, if so it be, \\
        We keep His words, "Remember Me," \\
        And then with joy Himself we see--- \\
        Jesus, our Lord.

\end{enumerate}

\begin{center}
    {\Large \textbf{HYMN 91\textdagger{}}}
    \\
    \textit{S.M. BOYLSTON}
\end{center}
\vspace{1em}

\begin{enumerate}
    \item WE bless Jehovah's name, \\
        Our sins are all forgiven; \\
        To suffer once to earth Christ came, \\
        And now He's crowned in heaven.

    \item His body offered up, \\
        His blood shed once for all, \\
        This broken loaf and poured-out cup \\
        So preciously recall.

    \item We never would forget \\
        His rich, unbounded love, \\
        Our theme of joy and wonder here, \\
        Our endless song above. Amen.

\end{enumerate}

\newpage
\begin{center}
    {\Large \textbf{HYMN 92\textdagger{}}}
    \\
    \textit{L.M. MELCOMBE}
\end{center}
\vspace{1em}

\begin{enumerate}
    \item WE never would forget His love, \\
        Who has redeemed us by His blood, \\
        And now as our High Priest above \\
        Lives as our Advocate with God.

    \item We never would forget the pain, \\
        The blood-like sweat, the shameful tree, \\
        The wrath His soul did once sustain, \\
        From sin and death to set us free.

    \item In broken bread and out-poured wine \\
        We hear Him say, "Remember Me; \\
        I gave My life, a price divine, \\
        I bore the wrath to set you free."

    \item O God, we gladly praise His love, \\
        Who suffered for us on the tree; \\
        And till He come from heaven above \\
        We keep His word---"Remember Me."

\end{enumerate}

\newpage
\begin{center}
    {\Large \textbf{HYMN 93\textdagger{}}}
    \\
    \textit{8.8.6.8.8.6. MARIENBERG}
\end{center}
\vspace{1em}

\begin{enumerate}
    \item TO Thee, O God, our voice we raise, \\
        And sing Thine everlasting praise, \\
        Confessing to Thy name. \\
        How great Thou art in holiness! \\
        What majesty Thou dost possess! \\
        We celebrate Thy fame.

    \item Thou, holy everlasting God, \\
        Didst sanctify us by the blood \\
        Of Thy belov\`ed Son. \\
        Raised from the dust of earth are we \\
        To be a priesthood unto Thee, \\
        Made holy in that One.

    \item We stand before Thee in His name; \\
        O God, we have no other claim \\
        To draw nigh unto Thee. \\
        But here in Him, our holy Lord, \\
        We praise Thy name with one accord; \\
        Accepted thus are we.

    \item Our praise ascends through Christ the Lord, \\
        According to Thy holy word; \\
        The great High Priest is He. \\
        The Spirit gives us power to raise \\
        Our heart and voice in holy praise, \\
        In holy praise to Thee.

    \item We praise Thee for the matchless grace \\
        Which gives us in Thy love a place \\
        And lifts us into heaven. \\
        All glory, honour, majesty \\
        Unto Thy name for ever be \\
        For love so freely given. Amen.

\end{enumerate}

\newpage
\begin{center}
    {\Large \textbf{HYMN 94\textdagger{}}}
    \\
    \textit{7.6.7.6. D. AURELIA}
\end{center}
\vspace{1em}

\begin{enumerate}
    \item THE Holies now we enter \\
        In perfect peace with God, \\
        Regaining the lost centre \\
        Through Christ's atoning blood; \\
        Though great may be our dulness \\
        In thought and word and deed, \\
        We glory in the fulness \\
        Of Him who meets our need.

    \item Much incense is ascending \\
        Before th' eternal throne; \\
        God graciously is bending \\
        To listen to His own. \\
        Though feeble are our praises \\
        Christ adds His sweet perfume, \\
        And love the censer raises \\
        Their odours to consume.

    \item O God, we come with singing \\
        Because the great High Priest \\
        Our names to Thee is bringing, \\
        Nor e'er forgets the least. \\
        For us He wears the mitre \\
        Where holiness shines bright; \\
        For us His robes are whiter \\
        Than heaven's unsullied light.

\end{enumerate}

\newpage
\begin{center}
    {\Large \textbf{HYMN 95\textdagger{}}}
    \\
    \textit{7.6.7.6. D. HEBER}
\end{center}
\vspace{1em}

\begin{enumerate}
    \item O BLESSED God, the Father \\
        Of Jesus Christ our Lord, \\
        In Him we are before Thee, \\
        According to Thy word; \\
        A kingdom He has made us, \\
        Priests unto Thee Most High, \\
        And we (as Thou hast bade us) \\
        As worshippers draw nigh.

    \item We meditate in wonder, \\
        O God, upon Thy love; \\
        Thy matchless grace we ponder \\
        That sent Christ from above; \\
        We think with joy, yet sadness, \\
        Of how He stooped to die, \\
        And praise Thy name with gladness \\
        He's seated now on high.

    \item We praise Thee for the savour \\
        Of His most bless\`ed name, \\
        Who brought such glory to Thee, \\
        Who did Thy love proclaim; \\
        He was for Thee an offering \\
        Devoted to Thy will; \\
        He gave Thee boundless pleasure, \\
        He Thee delighteth still.

\end{enumerate}

\begin{center}
    {\Large \textbf{HYMN 96\textdagger{}}}
    \\
    \textit{Irreg. WIR PFL\"{U}GEN}
\end{center}
\vspace{1em}

\begin{enumerate}
    \item INTO Thy holy presence, \\
        O God, we venture now, \\
        With reverent hearts and holy awe \\
        Before Thy throne to bow. \\
        We plead not our own virtues, \\
        They cannot here avail, \\
        But by the blood of Jesus \\
        We enter through the veil. \\
        For the gift Thou gavest, \\
        Thy Son from heaven above, \\
        We thank Thee, Lord, \\
        We praise Thee, Lord, \\
        Thou God of matchless love.

    \item No guilty fears assail us \\
        As those defiled with sin, \\
        But as a holy priesthood now \\
        With joy we enter in. \\
        Our great High Priest before us \\
        Has opened up the way, \\
        And clothed in His perfections \\
        We would our homage pay. \\
        For the gift Thou gavest, \\
        Thy Son from heaven above, \\
        We thank Thee, Lord, \\
        We praise Thee, Lord, \\
        Thou God of matchless love.

    \item Through Him our sacrifices \\
        Acceptable will be, \\
        Though not of earthly gifts received \\
        We tender unto Thee. \\
        To sacrifice of praises \\
        That unto Thee ascends, \\
        His bless\`ed name and person \\
        An incense-fragrance lends. \\
        For the gift Thou gavest, \\
        Thy Son from heaven above, \\
        We thank Thee, Lord, \\
        We praise Thee, Lord, \\
        Thou God of matchless love.

    \item We praise Thee and we worship, \\
        Thou great all-loving One, \\
        For giving up Thy Son to death \\
        For sins which we had done. \\
        His death and resurrection \\
        Have brought us nigh to Thee, \\
        Now with our hearts adoring \\
        To Thee we bow the knee. \\
        For the gift Thou gavest, \\
        Thy Son from heaven above, \\
        We thank Thee, Lord, \\
        We praise Thee, Lord, \\
        Thou God of matchless love. Amen.

\end{enumerate}

\newpage
\begin{center}
    {\Large \textbf{HYMN 97\textdagger{}}}
    \\
    \textit{8.7.8.7. D. AUSTRIA}
\end{center}
\vspace{1em}

\begin{enumerate}
    \item GATHERED in the name Lord Jesus, \\
        Breaking bread at His command, \\
        Looking at the mystic tokens \\
        We behold and understand \\
        How for us He left the glory \\
        As a Man 'mongst men to dwell, \\
        Gave His life-blood, wondrous story, \\
        Thus to rescue us from hell.

    \item Now with holy boldness bringing \\
        Firstfruits of our lives to God, \\
        Fruit of lips we join in singing \\
        Jesus' praise with one accord. \\
        Many hearts as one united, \\
        Many voices blend in one, \\
        As we raise our loud hosannas, \\
        Praises to our God alone.

    \item Jesus' death and resurrection \\
        Is the theme that fills our heart; \\
        On the cross for our redemption \\
        He was pierced with judgment's dart. \\
        For us stricken, smitten, bruis\`ed, \\
        For us bearing sin's vast load; \\
        All the wrath of God endur\`ed, \\
        Paid our debt with His life's blood.

    \item In the silent grave they lay Him, \\
        Seal the stone and guard it well. \\
        Sheol's fetters cannot hold Him, \\
        Powerless all the hosts of hell. \\
        He comes forth o'er death victorious, \\
        See Him now the risen One, \\
        Prince of life and King of glory, \\
        Raised to heaven's eternal throne.

    \item Prophet, Priest and King, behold Him, \\
        Saviour, Shepherd, Christ and Lord; \\
        Heav\`en's myriads bow before Him, \\
        Praise His name with one accord. \\
        Saints on earth their voices raising \\
        Shout the praises, bless the name \\
        Of the One who died to save us, \\
        Jesus evermore the same.

\end{enumerate}

\begin{center}
    {\Large \textbf{HYMN 98\textdagger{}}}
    \\
    \textit{12.13.12.11. TRINITY}
\end{center}
\vspace{1em}

\begin{enumerate}
    \item HOLY, holy, holy, we now come before Thee, \\
        Gathered in that holy name so very dear to Thee. \\
        O Lord God, we own Thee, humbly we adore Thee, \\
        For Thy grace and power and truth and love we see.

    \item Thou Thy Son hast giv\`en, see His body riv\`en, \\
        Pierced with cru\`el thorn and spear upon Golgotha's tree. \\
        For our sins He suffered, and His body offered, \\
        And His blood outpoured a sacrifice to Thee.

    \item Now through Jesus' merit, gathered by the Spirit, \\
        Here within the holy place, through Him we boldly come; \\
        Hearts and voices blending, praise to Thee ascending, \\
        Through our great High Priest we worship Thee alone.

    \item Holy, holy, holy, humbly we adore Thee, \\
        Honour, blessing, power and might we would ascribe to Thee; \\
        Courts of heav\`en ringing with the praise we're bringing, \\
        For the One who lives our living Lord to be.

\end{enumerate}

\newpage
\begin{center}
    {\Large \textbf{HYMN 99\textdagger{}}}
    \\
    \textit{L.M. MARYTON}
\end{center}
\vspace{1em}

\begin{enumerate}
    \item O LORD, Thy courts we humbly tread, \\
        By Thy blest Spirit hither led; \\
        We bring our sacrifice of praise, \\
        Adoring Thee in grateful lays.

    \item Chosen in Christ---oh, wondrous grace; \\
        Assured by Him a heavenly place! \\
        Ere worlds at Thy command were made, \\
        Salvation's wondrous plan was laid.

    \item How could Thy heart such love conceive--- \\
        To slay Thy Son and us receive? \\
        To charge with guilt the Holy One, \\
        Forsake Thy well-belov\`ed Son?

    \item The stream of love divine alone \\
        Through Christ could pardon and atone; \\
        Eternal ages it had flowed, \\
        And at the cross its fulness showed.

    \item Accepted through His death are we \\
        To be a priesthood, serving Thee. \\
        What joy is ours, through saving grace, \\
        To worship in the holy place!

    \item Thus boldly we now enter in, \\
        Cleansed by Christ's blood from every sin; \\
        It speaks for us before Thy throne, \\
        Proclaims redemption's work is done.

    \item Great God, Thy love all love excels; \\
        It humbles us, yet praise compels! \\
        Eternally our song shall be \\
        Of Him who said, "Remember Me."

\end{enumerate}

\begin{enumerate}
    \item BASKETS full of firstfruits, \\
        Sacrifice of praise--- \\
        Songs of our Redeemer \\
        Joyfully we raise. \\
        Lord, we come before Thee \\
        Through the living way, \\
        Worshipping with gladness \\
        On this holy day.

    \item All His deeds of kindness, \\
        Every act of grace \\
        Led Him but to Calvary \\
        And the outside place. \\
        There we see Him dying, \\
        Bowing to Thy will; \\
        Lamb of Thy providing, \\
        Loving sinners still!

    \item Bought with blood most precious, \\
        Pardoned through His death, \\
        Now a holy priesthood \\
        (Thus the scripture saith), \\
        In Thy holy presence \\
        We would humbly dwell; \\
        Hearts by Christ redeem\`ed \\
        Songs of praises swell.

    \item Lord, we only offer \\
        But a thousandth part \\
        Of the love that's owed Thee \\
        From each ransomed heart. \\
        Praise we now the Father, \\
        Praise be to the Son \\
        And the Holy Spirit--- \\
        Praise the Three in One.

\end{enumerate}

\newpage
\begin{center}
    {\Large \textbf{HYMN 101\textdagger{}}}
    \\
    \textit{7.6.7.6. D PENLAN}
\end{center}
\vspace{1em}

\begin{enumerate}
    \item WE come with hearts obedient, \\
        Our God, to worship Thee; \\
        The symbols we have taken \\
        In hallowed mem\`ory \\
        Of Christ, Thy Well-belov\`ed, \\
        The darling of Thy heart--- \\
        At Calv\`ary we see Him \\
        Pierced through with judgment's dart.

    \item We trace the way appointed \\
        That led to Calvary's hill, \\
        From birth to death obedient \\
        To Thy most holy will. \\
        Thyself in Him reflected, \\
        Immanuel, divine; \\
        O God, what grace and kindness \\
        In all His actions shine!

    \item Deep shadows crossed His pathway, \\
        Yet deeper still to be; \\
        We hear His cry of sorrow \\
        In dark Gethsemane. \\
        For He alone must suffer, \\
        Alone the cup must drain \\
        Of wrath and Thy forsaking, \\
        Our pardon to obtain.

    \item The Lamb of Thy providing \\
        Was on the altar laid; \\
        Our sins were laid upon Him, \\
        And thus our debt was paid. \\
        'Twas love divine that bound Him \\
        In death upon the tree, \\
        And, joined with sovereign mercy, \\
        Has set the captives free.

    \item Freed by such love, we ponder \\
        The fulness of His grace, \\
        That brings us in with gladness \\
        Within the holy place. \\
        O God, our hearts united \\
        Would praise Thee for Thy Son, \\
        For all in wondrous kindness \\
        That Thou, through Him, hast done. Amen.

\end{enumerate}
