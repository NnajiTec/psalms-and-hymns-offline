\begin{center}
    {\Large \textbf{HYMN 1}}
    \\
    \textit{C. M. TALLIS}
\end{center}
\vspace{1em}

\begin{enumerate}
    \item BLEST be the Wisdom and the Power, \\
        The Justice and the Grace, \\
        That joined in council to restore \\
        And save our ruined race.

    \item Our father ate forbidden fruit, \\
        And from his glory fell; \\
        And we his children thus were brought \\
        To death and near to hell.

    \item Blest be the Lord who sent His Son, \\
        To take our flesh and blood, \\
        To bring us life He gave His own, \\
        And made our peace with God.

    \item He honoured all His Father's laws \\
        Which we have disobeyed; \\
        He bore our sins upon the cross, \\
        And our full ransom paid.

    \item Behold Him rising from the grave, \\
        Behold Him raised on high; \\
        He pleads His merit there to save \\
        Transgressors doomed to die.

    \item There on a glorious throne He reigns, \\
        And by His power divine \\
        Redeems us from the slavish chains \\
        Of Satan and of sin.

    \item Thence soon the Son of God shall come, \\
        And with archangel voice \\
        Shall call His saints forth from the tomb \\
        While living saints rejoice.

\end{enumerate}

    {\Large \textbf{HYMN 2}}

    \textit{7.7.7.7. KELLY}

\vspace{1em}

\begin{enumerate}
    \item Lo! the infant Saviour lies, \\
        He appears in lowly guise, \\
        Yet by faith we read the words--- \\
        King of kings and Lord of lords.

    \item See! He stands at Pilate's bar, \\
        Most despised of all by far; \\
        Still to Him belong the words--- \\
        King of kings and Lord of lords.

    \item He who wears the crown of thorns, \\
        He whom man reviles and scorns, \\
        Yet demands as His the words--- \\
        King of kings and Lord of lords.

    \item On the cross 'tis still the same, \\
        Never can He yield His claim \\
        To those ever glorious words--- \\
        King of kings and Lord of lords.

    \item Past the conflict of His love, \\
        See He takes His place above; \\
        On His vesture shine the words--- \\
        King of kings and Lord of lords.

\end{enumerate}

\newpage
\begin{center}
    {\Large \textbf{HYMN 3}}
    \\
    \textit{8.7.8.7.4.7. NEANDER}
\end{center}
\vspace{1em}

\begin{enumerate}
    \item ON this Lordly day assembled, \\
        Gathered by the call of God, \\
        We rejoice, though once we trembled, \\
        Fearing His avenging rod; \\
        Christ redeemed us; \\
        Let us trace the path He trod:

    \item In the Babe in David's city, \\
        Lowly in a manger laid, \\
        We behold the Lord Almighty, \\
        Him by whom the world was made. \\
        What a wonder, \\
        God in human form arrayed!

    \item Mark the earnest Workman toiling, \\
        Faithful to His task and true, \\
        Deeming nought of labour soiling \\
        That His kingly hands would do. \\
        Man so perfect; \\
        Holy, noble, humble too.

    \item View the Man of Sorrows, treading \\
        Earth's dark vale of sin and woe, \\
        Love and healing mercy spreading \\
        Everywhere His footsteps go. \\
        Pattern Servant, \\
        Doing all God's will below.

    \item Now upon the cross suspended, \\
        See the Saviour crucified; \\
        Slain by men His love befriended, \\
        Who His agonies deride. \\
        Yet for sinners, \\
        'Neath the wrath of God He died.

    \item His atoning work completed, \\
        Now He lives no more to die; \\
        On the Father's throne He's seated, \\
        See Him victor-crowned on high. \\
        Hallelujah! \\
        'Tis by Him we now draw nigh.

    \item For His birth and life obedient \\
        To Thy perfect holy will, \\
        For His death, no mere expedient, \\
        But Thy counsels to fulfil, \\
        We Thy people, \\
        Bless\`ed God, would praise Thee still.

    \item Praise the Father, for He gave us \\
        Gift most precious in His Son. \\
        Praise the Son, for He to save us \\
        Such a mighty work has done. \\
        Praise the Spirit \\
        Who revealed it; \\
        Praise Jehovah, Three in One. \\
        Amen.

\end{enumerate}

\newpage
\begin{center}
    {\Large \textbf{HYMN 4}}
    \\
    \textit{8.7.8.7. ALL FOR JESUS}
\end{center}
\vspace{1em}

\begin{enumerate}
    \item FROM the realm of light and glory \\
        Came the Son of God as Man, \\
        To unfold the wondrous story \\
        Veiled in God's eternal plan.

    \item Leaving heav\`en's throne of splendour, \\
        Stepping down to earth's dark night--- \\
        Shepherds find Him in a manger, \\
        Gaze in wonder at the sight!

    \item Woman's Seed in Eden promised, \\
        God incarnate, virgin-born; \\
        Morning Star by men unnoticed, \\
        Herald of a glorious dawn.

    \item As Jehovah's Servant treading \\
        Sin-stained earth with heavenly grace, \\
        Ever onward, never faltering--- \\
        All His steps we love to trace.

    \item On the cross, His will unshaken, \\
        Dying, outcast, crushed, alone; \\
        Visage marred, by God forsaken, \\
        There for sinners to atone.

    \item Victor over death and Hades, \\
        Foes all vanquished 'neath His feet, \\
        To the Father's side He rises, \\
        There to take the highest seat!

    \item Tribute bring we to the Father \\
        For the Son, His gift of love; \\
        Bowing low, our praise we offer \\
        In the holy place above.

\end{enumerate}

\newpage
\begin{center}
    {\Large \textbf{HYMN 5}}
    \\
    \textit{C.M.D. BLESSED NAME}
\end{center}
\vspace{1em}

\begin{enumerate}
    \item WE bless and praise Thee, gracious God, \\
        For giving Thine own Son, \\
        Who did partake of flesh and blood, \\
        In all our sorrows one. \\
        Well may we wonder at the thought \\
        That Christ came down so low, \\
        That He so near to us was brought \\
        To understand our woe.

    \item He, mingling with the human race, \\
        Saw sin, death, Satan's power; \\
        Before His holy piercing gaze \\
        The very demons cower. \\
        But not alone in living strife \\
        He fought the triple foe; \\
        Upon the cross He gave His life; \\
        By dying laid them low.

    \item For us He died upon the tree, \\
        For us was shed His blood, \\
        But more than that since unto Thee \\
        He gave Himself, O God. \\
        Christ, spotless, offered Thee Himself, \\
        Oh, what a gift divine! \\
        Its fragrant worth no tongue can tell, \\
        What joy, O God, was Thine!

    \item In the eternal Spirit He \\
        Offered Himself to God; \\
        And in the Holy Spirit we \\
        Now sing a grateful ode. \\
        For now He lives, we praise Thy name, \\
        He lives no more to die; \\
        'Tis ours to celebrate His fame, \\
        Who sits with Thee on high.

    \item Before Thy face we own His grace, \\
        He once for us was poor. \\
        We bless His name, we sing His fame, \\
        He liveth evermore. \\
        Our hymn of praise with joy we raise, \\
        His worth no tongue can tell. \\
        So great His love all praise above, \\
        He hath done all things well.

\end{enumerate}

\newpage
\begin{center}
    {\Large \textbf{HYMN 6}}
    \\
    \textit{7.6.7.6. ST. ALPHEGE}
\end{center}
\vspace{1em}

\begin{enumerate}
    \item SENT from th' eternal Father \\
        Who dwells in light above, \\
        Came forth the Son most holy \\
        To manifest His love.

    \item This is the wondrous story--- \\
        Born of a Hebrew maid \\
        The Lord of life and glory; \\
        And in a manger laid.

    \item He grew in human stature, \\
        In every childlike grace; \\
        He lived a life most holy \\
        In all His youthful days;

    \item Attaining manhood's ripeness, \\
        Midst sinners sinless He; \\
        And though in human likeness, \\
        From human errors free.

    \item He entered on His life-work, \\
        Approved by God Most High; \\
        When tempted in the desert \\
        He made the Tempter fly.

    \item In Galilee behold Him \\
        A simple wedding-guest, \\
        But mighty powers enfold Him, \\
        Believing ones are blest.

    \item Through this dark vale of sorrow \\
        He, clothed with pity, went. \\
        In Him each sick and sad one \\
        Found power and mercy blent.

    \item But looming black before Him \\
        See His dread cross appear; \\
        Oh, well may we adore Him, \\
        He bore our sorrows there!

    \item Then glory to the Father, \\
        For us His Son He gave; \\
        To Christ, Kinsman-Redeemer, \\
        He died our souls to save;

    \item To God th' eternal Spirit, \\
        'Tis He reveals the Son; \\
        We praise Thy holy merit, \\
        Jehovah, Three in One.

\end{enumerate}

    \textit{L.M. BOSTON}

\begin{enumerate}
    \item 'Tis midnight, and on Olive's brow \\
        The star is dimmed that lately shone; \\
        'Tis midnight in the garden now, \\
        The suffering Saviour prays alone.

    \item 'Tis midnight, and from all removed \\
        The Saviour wrestles 'lone with fears; \\
        E'en that disciple whom He loved \\
        Heeds not his Master's grief and tears.

    \item 'Tis midnight, agonized He kneels, \\
        The sweat falls down like drops of blood; \\
        Yet He who such deep anguish feels \\
        Is not forsaken by His God.

    \item 'Tis midnight, but before Him loom \\
        Yet darker hours of deeper woe; \\
        Golgotha's anguish, death, the tomb, \\
        The patient Saviour needs must know.

    \item O God, those midnight scenes of woe \\
        In tenderest memory we recall; \\
        We bless the One who loved us so, \\
        And fully own that Christ is all.

\end{enumerate}

    {\Large \textbf{HYMN 12*}}
    \\
    \textit{8.6.8.6.8.6. SPOHR}
\end{center}
\vspace{1em}

\begin{enumerate}
    \item How deep and grievous was the woe \\
        Of Christ upon the cross! \\
        It laid the mighty Saviour low \\
        When hanging there for us. \\
        To bring us gain He bare the pain, \\
        And suffered shame and loss.

    \item The wage of sin was in our cup, \\
        Wrath filled it to the brim; \\
        Its bitterness we'll never sup, \\
        That cup was drunk by Him. \\
        'Twas all for us He suffered thus, \\
        That we might live through Him.

    \item There rose a keen relentless storm, \\
        It burst on Christ alone; \\
        It marred His visage and His form, \\
        But thus He saved His own. \\
        He bore the grief for our relief, \\
        Unaided and alone.

    \item Jehovah's righteous anger dire \\
        Fell on His only Son; \\
        He bare the brunt of all God's ire, \\
        And thus our peace was won. \\
        The stripes were sore the Saviour bore; \\
        God spar\`ed not His Son.

    \item His wrathful sword was raised to smite \\
        The One who pleased Him well; \\
        On Him in whom He had delight \\
        The bitter chastening fell. \\
        Our peace He made, the price He paid; \\
        His love no tongue can tell.

    \item It was for us Christ Jesus died, \\
        A Victim on the cross, \\
        Revealing thus the boundless tide \\
        Of love He has to us. \\
        He is our Lord alone adored \\
        Who died upon the cross.

    \item The One who suffered grief and shame \\
        Is raised and glorified. \\
        We sing the triumph of His name \\
        Who groaned and bled and died. \\
        We celebrate His triumph great; \\
        He's raised and glorified.

\end{enumerate}

\begin{center}
    {\Large \textbf{HYMN 17*}}
    \\
    \textit{6.6.6.6.8.8. HUSHED WAS THE EVENING HYMN}
\end{center}
\vspace{1em}

\begin{enumerate}
    \item HIMSELF He could not save, \\
        He on the cross must die, \\
        Or mercy could not come \\
        To ruined sinners nigh. \\
        Yes, Christ the Son of God must bleed, \\
        That sinners might from sin be freed.

    \item Himself He could not save, \\
        For justice must be done, \\
        Our sins' full weight must fall \\
        Upon the sinless One. \\
        For nothing less could God accept \\
        In payment of that fearful debt.

    \item Himself He could not save, \\
        Love's stream too deeply flowed, \\
        In love Himself He gave \\
        To pay the debt we owed. \\
        Obedience to the Father's will \\
        And love to Him did all fulfil.

    \item Himself He could not save, \\
        But now exalted high, \\
        A Prince and Saviour He \\
        Has saved and brought us nigh. \\
        We live in Him who lives above, \\
        And sing with joy His deathless love.

\end{enumerate}

        Until we hence are taken \\
        To see Him face to face.
