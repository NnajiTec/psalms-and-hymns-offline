\begin{center}
    {\Large \textbf{HYMN 67}}
    \\
    \textit{7.6.7.6. D. ANWOTH}
\end{center}
\vspace{1em}

\begin{enumerate}
    \item ERE God had built the mountains, \\
        Or raised the fruitful hills; \\
        Before He filled the fountains \\
        That feed the running rills; \\
        In One from everlasting, \\
        The wonderful I AM \\
        Found pleasures never wasting, \\
        And Wisdom was His name.

    \item When, like a tent to dwell in, \\
        He spread the skies abroad, \\
        And swathed about the swelling \\
        Of ocean's mighty flood, \\
        He wrought by weight and measure; \\
        Wisdom was with Him then; \\
        Himself the Father's pleasure, \\
        And His the sons of men.

    \item Thus Wisdom's words discover \\
        His glory and His grace, \\
        The everlasting Lover \\
        Of our unworthy race. \\
        His gracious eye surveyed us \\
        Ere stars were seen above; \\
        In wisdom He has made us, \\
        And died for us in love.

    \item The Lord is now delighted \\
        To have us for His own, \\
        The love no longer slighted \\
        Which He to us has shown; \\
        In spite of all our badness, \\
        Oh, miracle of grace! \\
        He brings us in with gladness \\
        Before His Father's face.

\end{enumerate}

\newpage
\begin{center}
    {\Large \textbf{HYMN 68}}
    \\
    \textit{8.6.8.6.8.8. SUPREMACY}
    \\
    \textit{\small [The last line to be repeated]}
\end{center}
\vspace{1em}

\begin{enumerate}
    \item WE laud the everlasting Word, \\
        The Father's only Son, \\
        God manifestly seen and heard, \\
        And heaven's belov\`ed One. \\
        Worthy the name of Jesus now \\
        That every knee therein should bow.

    \item In Him, most perfectly expressed, \\
        The Father's glories shine; \\
        Of the full Deity possessed, \\
        Eternally divine. \\
        Worthy the name of Jesus now \\
        That every knee therein should bow.

    \item True image of the Infinite \\
        Whose essence is concealed; \\
        Brightness of uncreated light, \\
        The heart of God revealed. \\
        Worthy the name of Jesus now \\
        That every knee therein should bow.

    \item But the high mysteries of His name \\
        An angel's grasp transcend; \\
        The Father only (glorious claim) \\
        The Son can comprehend. \\
        Worthy the name of Jesus now \\
        That every knee therein should bow.

    \item Yet loving Him, on whom God's love \\
        Ineffable doth rest, \\
        His members all---below, above--- \\
        As one with Him are blest. \\
        Worthy the name of Jesus now \\
        That every knee therein should bow.

    \item Of the vast universe of bliss \\
        The centre He and sun; \\
        Th' eternal theme of praise is this--- \\
        God's well-belov\`ed One. \\
        Worthy the name of Jesus now \\
        That every knee therein should bow.

\end{enumerate}

\newpage
\begin{center}
    {\Large \textbf{HYMN 69}}
    \\
    \textit{L.M.D. CREATION.}
\end{center}
\vspace{1em}

\begin{enumerate}
    \item COME, let us sing this song of love, \\
        Of Him who came from heaven above; \\
        He made the worlds by His great power, \\
        And keeps them till the final hour. \\
        Creation doth His word obey, \\
        And none can stay His hand or say \\
        "What doest Thou? Thy wonders cease." \\
        He is the God of power and peace.

    \item He rules through heaven and earth and hell, \\
        His creatures all His wonders tell; \\
        In His own hand He holds the seas, \\
        He guides the tide, controls the breeze; \\
        The frost, the snow, the ice, the cold, \\
        All are by His great power controlled; \\
        And yet an infant Babe He lay \\
        In Bethlehem's manger on that day.

    \item The One who dwells in heaven so high \\
        We see within the manger lie; \\
        Th' eternal God, a little Child, \\
        So gentle, quiet, meek and mild. \\
        He came His Father to obey, \\
        Our sins by death to put away; \\
        Came to redeem us and to save \\
        From sin, from death and from the grave.

    \item To God our joyful praise we bring; \\
        His love, His power to save we sing. \\
        Our hearts adore Thee now, O God; \\
        We trace with joy the path He trod. \\
        With heart and voice we sing this day, \\
        And humbly we our homage pay; \\
        We wait to see His glorious face, \\
        And evermore to sing Thy grace.

\end{enumerate}

\newpage
\begin{center}
    {\Large \textbf{HYMN 70}}
    \\
    \textit{8.7.8.7.4.7. PRAISE, MY SOUL}
\end{center}
\vspace{1em}

\begin{enumerate}
    \item GLORY, glory everlasting \\
        Be to Him who bare the cross, \\
        Who redeemed our souls by tasting \\
        Death, the death deserved by us! \\
        Spread His glory \\
        Who redeemed His people thus.

    \item His is love, 'tis love unbounded, \\
        Without measure, without end; \\
        Human thought is here confounded, \\
        'Tis too great to comprehend. \\
        Praise the Saviour, \\
        Magnify the sinner's Friend.

    \item While we hear the wondrous story \\
        Of the Saviour's cross and shame, \\
        Sing we, "Everlasting glory \\
        Be to God and to the Lamb." \\
        Hallelujah! \\
        Give ye glory to His name.

\end{enumerate}

\newpage
\begin{center}
    {\Large \textbf{HYMN 71*}}
    \\
    \textit{C.M. BELMONT}
\end{center}
\vspace{1em}

\begin{enumerate}
    \item IN Christ the Lord our eyes behold \\
        A thousand glories more \\
        Than the rich gems and polished gold \\
        The sons of Aaron wore.

    \item They first their own sin-offering brought \\
        To purge themselves from sin; \\
        His life was pure, without a spot, \\
        And all His nature clean.

    \item Fresh blood as constant as the day \\
        Was on their altars spilt, \\
        But His one offering takes away \\
        For ever all our guilt.

    \item Their priesthood passed through several hands, \\
        For mortal was their race; \\
        His never-changing office stands \\
        Eternal as His days.

    \item Their range was earth, nor higher soared, \\
        The heaven of heavens is His; \\
        There, in His majesty, the Lord \\
        A Priest for ever is.

    \item Eternal glories crown His name, \\
        As Prophet, Priest, and King; \\
        Soon heaven and earth shall sound His fame, \\
        Each day fresh praises bring.

\end{enumerate}

    \\
    \textit{L.M. SAMSON}
\end{center}

\begin{enumerate}
    \item AWAKE each soul in joyful lays \\
        And sing the great Redeemer's praise. \\
        He justly claims our melody; \\
        His loving-kindness, oh, how free!

    \item He saw us ruined in the Fall, \\
        Yet loved us notwithstanding all. \\
        He saved us from our lost estate; \\

\end{enumerate}

\newpage
\begin{center}
    {\Large \textbf{HYMN 75}}
    \\
    \textit{8.8.8.8.8.8. EATON}
\end{center}
\vspace{1em}

\begin{enumerate}
    \item JESUS! that name all names above, \\
        Sweet passport of eternal love, \\
        Unfolding to a fallen race \\
        The wealth of God's infinite grace. \\
        What mind can grasp, what tongue proclaim \\
        The value of that peerless name?

    \item Jesus! a note of sweeter sound \\
        Not e'en in heaven itself is found; \\
        It thrills with joy the heart of God, \\
        This name of the incarnate Word. \\
        What mind can grasp, what tongue proclaim \\
        The sweetness of that peerless name?

    \item Jesus! the name that ne'er can fail \\
        To make the powers of darkness quail; \\
        To fear-wrung hearts gives lasting peace, \\
        Sin-burdened souls complete release. \\
        What mind can grasp, what tongue proclaim \\
        The prowess of that peerless name?

    \item Jesus! the name which can impart \\
        True solace to each sorrowing heart; \\
        Of death subdue the sting and gloom, \\
        With heavenly glory gild the tomb. \\
        What mind can grasp, what tongue proclaim \\
        The comfort of that peerless name?

    \item Jesus! the name through which alone \\
        Believing ones approach the throne; \\
        To God their hearts' desires outpour \\
        And draw from heaven's exhaustless store. \\
        What mind can grasp, what tongue proclaim \\
        The riches of that peerless name?

    \item Jesus! the name in which shall bow \\
        Each knee in heaven, on earth, below; \\
        God glorify with one accord, \\
        Confessing Jesus Christ as Lord. \\
        May we each day by day proclaim \\
        The glory of that peerless name.

    \item And when we pass to realms above, \\
        See Him who taught us heavenly love, \\
        With ransomed hosts enraptured raise \\
        Triumphant songs of ceaseless praise, \\
        Unhindered then shall we proclaim \\
        The virtues of that peerless name!

\end{enumerate}

\newpage
\begin{center}
    {\Large \textbf{HYMN 76}}
    \\
    \textit{8.6.8.6.8.8.8.8. KARENS.}
\end{center}
\vspace{1em}

\begin{enumerate}
    \item O GOD, what perfect rest is Thine! \\
        Thy rest is in Thy Son; \\
        'Tis all unspeakable, divine, \\
        Thy rest and ours are one. \\
        Inside the circle of Thy love \\
        Joined to His life are we above; \\
        How sweet with Thee, O God, to share \\
        The joy which is Thy portion there!

    \item Thou findest in that spotless One, \\
        Where all perfections dwell, \\
        All that Thy heart could wish for us, \\
        All that Thou e'er couldst tell; \\
        Thou findest ransomed, righteous, fair, \\
        Where all Thy joys transcendent are, \\
        In Him a people for Thy praise, \\
        Thy glory through eternal days.

\end{enumerate}

    {\Large \textbf{HYMN 77}}
    \\
    \textit{6.6.8.6.8.8. PISGAH.}
\end{center}
\vspace{1em}

\begin{enumerate}
    \item O GOD of matchless grace \\
        We sing unto Thy name; \\
        We stand accepted in the place \\
        That none but Christ could claim. \\
        Our willing hearts have heard Thy voice, \\
        And in Thy mercy we rejoice.

    \item 'Tis meet that Thy delight \\
        Should centre in Thy Son; \\
        That Thou shouldst place us in Thy sight \\
        In Him Thy Holy One. \\
        Thy perfect love hath cast out fear, \\
        Thy favour shines upon us here.

    \item Eternal is our rest \\
        In Him who pleas\`ed Thee; \\
        Now of His peace, His joy, possessed, \\
        We wait His face to see. \\
        Now to Thy heart, O God, received, \\
        We know Thee whom we have believed.

\end{enumerate}

    \textit{8.8.6.8.8.6. PIETY.}

\begin{enumerate}
    \item COME, let us sing the matchless worth, \\
        And sweetly sound the praises forth, \\
        Which in the Saviour shine; \\
        To God our praise of Christ we'll bring, \\
        The song with which the heav\`ens ring \\
        We'll join through grace divine.

    \item How rich that precious blood once spilt, \\
        Our rescue from the dreadful guilt \\
        Of sin against our God! \\
        How perfect is the righteousness, \\
        In which unspotted, beauteous dress \\

    \item Great are the offices He bears, \\
        And bright His character appears \\
        Exalted on the throne. \\
        In songs of sweet, untiring praise \\
        We would to everlasting days \\

    \item And soon the happy day shall come \\
        When we shall reach our destined home, \\
        And see Him face to face; \\
        Then with our Saviour, Master, Friend, \\
        The glad eternity we'll spend, \\
        And celebrate God's grace.

\end{enumerate}

\begin{center}
    {\Large \textbf{HYMN 79}}
    \\
    \textit{7.6.7.6. D. LYMINGTON.}
\end{center}
\vspace{1em}

\begin{enumerate}
    \item BY Thee, O God, invited \\
        We look unto the Son, \\
        In whom Thy soul delighted, \\
        Who all Thy will hath done; \\
        And by the one chief treasure \\
        Thy bosom freely gave, \\
        Thine own pure love we measure, \\
        Thy willing mind to save.

    \item O gracious God the Father, \\
        The one unchanging claim, \\
        The brightest hopes we gather \\
        From Christ's most precious name. \\
        What always sounds so sweetly \\
        In Thine unwearied ear, \\
        Hath freed our souls completely \\
        From all our sinful fear.

    \item The trembling sinner feareth \\
        That God can ne'er forget, \\
        But one full payment cleareth \\
        His mem'ry of all debt. \\
        When nought beside could ease us \\
        Or set our souls at large, \\
        The precious blood of Jesus \\
        Secured a full discharge.

    \item No wrath God's heart retaineth \\
        To usward who believe; \\
        No dread in ours remaineth, \\
        As we His love receive. \\
        Returning ones He kisses, \\
        And with His robe invests, \\
        His perfect love dismisses \\
        All terror from our breasts.

\end{enumerate}

    \\
    \textit{7.6.7.6. D. MORNING LIGHT}

\vspace{1em}

\begin{enumerate}
    \item WE sing with exultation \\
        To Thee, eternal God, \\
        Of Him who's Thy salvation, \\
        Of Jesus Christ our Lord. \\
        'Twas He who once descended \\
        More marred than men to be, \\
        Than men whom He befriended, \\
        Who pierced Him on the tree.

    \item Through Him Thy love's commended \\
        And justice satisfied; \\
        To Thee He hath ascended \\
        Whom He hath glorified; \\
        And now Thy love unbounded \\
        Is flowing full and free, \\
        And Him Thou hast surrounded \\
        With brightest majesty.

    \item Soon He will be descending \\
        For us into the air, \\
        And we in clouds ascending \\
        Caught up unto Him there; \\
        He will with exultation \\
        Set us before Thy throne \\
        Of highest exaltation \\
        Midst glories yet unknown.

\end{enumerate}

\begin{center}
    {\Large \textbf{HYMN 81\textdagger{}}}
    \\
    \textit{10.4.10.7.4.10. WONDERFUL LOVE}
\end{center}
\vspace{1em}

\begin{enumerate}
    \item LORD, we adore Him who came from Thy side \\
        Long, long ago, \\
        Leaving the glory that heaven could provide, \\
        Coming to suffering and woe. \\
        Jesus our Lord--- \\
        Wonderful gift of the Father of love.

    \item We will remember Him, keeping His word--- \\
        "This must ye do;" \\
        Gladly discerning His body, O Lord, \\
        Taught by the Spirit 'tis true. \\
        Jesus our Lord--- \\
        Sacrificed, bearing our sins on the tree.

    \item We will remember His coming to earth, \\
        Immanuel! \\
        There as a Babe we behold Him at birth, \\
        God in this world here to dwell. \\
        Jesus our Lord--- \\
        Laid in a manger, eternally God!

    \item We will remember His wonderful life, \\
        Doing Thy will. \\
        Perfect obedience midst sorrow and strife \\
        Led Him to Calv\`ary's hill. \\
        Jesus our Lord--- \\
        Loving and giving each step of the way.

    \item We will remember His death on the tree, \\
        Blest be His name! \\
        Suffering the just for the unjust was He; \\
        'Twas for this purpose He came. \\
        Jesus our Lord--- \\
        Giving Himself, our Redeemer to be.

    \item We will remember He rose from the dead, \\
        Mighty to save. \\
        Living for ever, our glorious Head \\
        Conquered the power of the grave. \\
        Jesus our Lord--- \\
        Praises for Him we present unto Thee.

\end{enumerate}

\newpage
\begin{center}
    {\Large \textbf{HYMN 83}}
    \\
    \textit{L.M.D. NEWCASTLE}
\end{center}
\vspace{1em}

\begin{enumerate}
    \item THE countless multitude on high \\
        Who tune their songs to Jesus' name, \\
        All merit of their own deny, \\
        And Jesus' worth alone proclaim. \\
        Firm on the ground of sovereign grace \\
        They stand before Jehovah's throne; \\
        The only song in that blest place \\
        Is, "Thou art worthy, Thou alone!"

    \item Salvation's glory all be paid \\
        To Him who sits upon the throne; \\
        And to the Lamb, whose blood was shed--- \\
        "Thou, Thou art worthy, Thou alone; \\
        For Thou wast slain, and in Thy blood \\
        These robes were washed so spotless pure; \\
        Thou mad'st us kings and priests to God, \\
        For ever let Thy praise endure."

    \item Let us with joy adopt the strain \\
        We hope to sing for ever there--- \\
        "Worthy the Lamb for sinners slain, \\
        Worthy alone the crown to wear!" \\
        Without one thought that's good to plead, \\
        Oh, what could shield us from despair, \\
        But this---though we are vile indeed, \\
        The Lord our righteousness is there?

\end{enumerate}

\newpage
\begin{center}
    {\Large \textbf{HYMN 84}}
    \\
    \textit{8.8.6.8.8.6. MANNA}
\end{center}
\vspace{1em}

\begin{enumerate}
    \item THOUGH all the beasts that live and feed \\
        Upon a thousand hills should bleed, \\
        Though all their blood should flow, \\
        The sacrifice would be in vain; \\
        The stain of sin would still remain; \\
        Sin is not cancelled so.

    \item A better sacrifice than these \\
        It needs, the conscience to appease, \\
        And satisfy the Lord. \\
        No blood hath virtue to atone \\
        For man's offence, but His alone \\
        Whose title is the Word.

    \item Jesus the Christ, on earth His name, \\
        He came, in love to sinners came, \\
        And bowed His head and died. \\
        A full atonement now is made, \\
        The ransom by His death is paid, \\
        And justice satisfied.

    \item That sinners might draw near to Him, \\
        God planned this great, this wondrous scheme, \\
        And found the ransom too. \\
        Let all the saints their voices raise, \\
        And sing the great Redeemer's praise, \\
        While endless ages flow.

\end{enumerate}
