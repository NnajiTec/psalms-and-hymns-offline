\begin{center}
    {\Large \textbf{HYMN 146\textdagger{}}}
    \\
    \textit{10.10.10.10. EVENTIDE}
    \\
    \textit{\small [Moderately fast]}
\end{center}
\vspace{1em}

\begin{enumerate}
    \item HERE, bless\`ed God, we see Him face to face; \\
        Here faith can touch and handle things unseen; \\
        Here would we grasp with firmer hands His grace, \\
        And all our weariness upon Him lean.

    \item Here would we feed upon the bread of God, \\
        Here drink with Him the royal wine of heaven; \\
        Here would we lay aside each earthly load, \\
        Here taste afresh the calm of sin forgiven.

    \item We have no help but His, nor do we need \\
        Another arm save His to lean upon, \\
        It is enough, O God, enough indeed, \\
        Our strength is in His might, His might alone.

    \item Ours is the sin, but His the righteousness, \\
        Ours is the guilt, but His the cleansing blood. \\
        Here is our robe, our refuge, and our peace, \\
        The righteousness He wrought, O Lord our God.

    \item Too soon we rise, the symbols disappear, \\
        The feast, though not the love, is past and gone; \\
        The bread and wine remove, but He is here, \\
        Nearer than ever, still our Shield and Sun.

    \item Feast after feast thus comes and passes by, \\
        Yet passing points to the glad feast above, \\
        Giving sweet foretastes of the festal joy, \\
        The Lamb's great bridal feast of bliss and love.

\end{enumerate}

\newpage
\begin{center}
    {\Large \textbf{HYMN 147}}
    \\
    \textit{L.M. ST. AIDAN (German)}
    \\
    \textit{\small [Slow]}
\end{center}
\vspace{1em}

\begin{enumerate}
    \item AMEN, one lasting, long Amen--- \\
        Blest anthem of eternal days; \\
        The fulness of the rapturous song \\
        To Christ the Saviour's endless praise.

    \item Amen, one lasting, long Amen--- \\
        Heaven's blissful cadence, deep and loud, \\
        While every heart before the throne \\
        In holy, solemn awe is bowed.

    \item Amen, Amen, it rolls along, \\
        Re-echoing from the throne again; \\
        Be ours to mingle with the throng \\
        In that eternal, loud Amen.

\end{enumerate}

\newpage
\begin{center}
    {\Large \textbf{HYMN 148}}
    \\
    \textit{6.6.6.6.4.4.4.4. RICHMOND}
    \\
    \textit{\small [Moderately fast]}
\end{center}
\vspace{1em}

\begin{enumerate}
    \item LORD of the worlds above, \\
        How pleasant and how fair \\
        The dwellings of Thy love, \\
        The heavenly mansions are! \\
        To Thine abode \\
        Our hearts aspire \\
        With warm desire \\
        To see our God.

    \item There is Thy throne of grace, \\
        And there the sprinkled blood; \\
        There lives before Thy face \\
        Our great High Priest, O God; \\
        His name our plea, \\
        We now draw near \\
        In holy fear \\
        To worship Thee.

    \item We go from strength to strength \\
        Through this dark vale of tears, \\
        Till each arrives at length \\
        And safe in heaven appears. \\
        Oh, glorious seat, \\
        Where God the King \\
        Shall shortly bring \\
        Our willing feet!

\end{enumerate}

\newpage
\begin{center}
    {\Large \textbf{HYMN 149}}
    \\
    \textit{7.7.7.7. WEBER}
    \\
    \textit{\small [Moderate]}
\end{center}
\vspace{1em}

\begin{enumerate}
    \item CHRIST has done the mighty work; \\
        Nothing left for us to do \\
        But to enter on His toil, \\
        Enter on His triumph too.

    \item His the pardon, ours the sin--- \\
        Great the sin, the pardon great; \\
        His the good, and ours the ill; \\
        His the love, and ours the hate.

    \item His the labour, ours the r\`est; \\
        His the death, and ours the life; \\
        Ours the fruits of vict\`ory, \\
        His the agony and strife.

    \item He has sown the precious seed, \\
        Nothing left for us unsown; \\
        Ours it is to reap the field, \\
        Make the harvest joy our own.

\end{enumerate}

\newpage
\begin{center}
    {\Large \textbf{HYMN 150}}
    \\
    \textit{10.10.10.10. PAX DEI}
    \\
    \textit{\small [Moderate]}
\end{center}
\vspace{1em}

\begin{enumerate}
    \item JEHOVAH God the Father, bless and keep \\
        His little flock of feeble lambs and sheep; \\
        Jehovah God the Son, His glorious face \\
        Make shine on us, so full of kindly grace;

    \item Jehovah God the Paraclete, uplift \\
        His countenance, and grant us peace, His gift. \\
        Eternal triune God, with love inflame \\
        Our souls, to praise Thy great Jehovah name. Amen, amen.

\end{enumerate}

\newpage
\begin{center}
    {\Large \textbf{HYMN 151}}
    \\
    \textit{6.6.6.6. QUAM DILECTA}
    \\
    \textit{\small [Moderate]}
\end{center}
\vspace{1em}

\begin{enumerate}
    \item ONCE more before we part, \\
        We bless the Saviour's name; \\
        Let every tongue and heart \\
        Join to extol the Lamb.

    \item We on Thy holy word \\
        Would feed, and live, and grow; \\
        Go on to know Thee, Lord, \\
        And practise what we know.

    \item Lord, in Thy grace we came; \\
        Thy blessing still impart. \\
        We met in Thine own name \\
        And in Thy name we part.

\end{enumerate}

\newpage
\begin{center}
    {\Large \textbf{HYMN 152}}
    \\
    \textit{C.M. JACKSON'S}
    \\
    \textit{\small [Moderately fast]}
\end{center}
\vspace{1em}

\begin{enumerate}
    \item THIS is the day the Lord hath made, \\
        He calls the hours His own. \\
        Let heaven rejoice, let earth be glad, \\
        And praise surround Thy throne.

    \item To-day He rose and left the dead, \\
        And Satan's empire fell; \\
        To-day the saints His triumph spread, \\
        And all His wonders tell.

    \item Help us, O Lord, on this blest day, \\
        To spread the glad, good news, \\
        And may Thy gospel's healing ray \\
        Its saving power diffuse.

    \item Hosanna to th' anointed King, \\
        To God's most holy Son! \\
        With gladsome hearts we now would bring \\
        Our praise unto the throne.

\end{enumerate}
