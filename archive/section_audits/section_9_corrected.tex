    {\Large \textbf{HYMN 157}}
    \\
    \textit{L.M.D. PETERBOROUGH}
    \\
    \textit{\small [Moderate]}
\end{center}
\vspace{1em}

\begin{enumerate}
    \item THE spacious firmament on high, \\
        With all the blue ethereal sky, \\
        And spangled heavens, a shining frame, \\
        Their great Original proclaim. \\
        Th' unwearied sun from day to day \\
        Does his Creator's power display, \\
        And publishes to every land \\
        The work of an almighty hand.

    \item Soon as the evening shades prevail \\
        The moon takes up the wondrous tale, \\
        And nightly to the listening earth \\
        Repeats the story of her birth; \\
        While all the stars that round her burn, \\
        And all the planets in their turn, \\
        Confirm the tidings as they roll, \\
        And spread the truth from pole to pole.

    \item What though in solemn silence all \\
        Move round the dark terrestrial ball? \\
        What though no real voice or sound \\
        Amidst their radiant orbs be found? \\
        In reason's ear they all rejoice \\
        And utter forth a glorious voice; \\
        For ever singing as they shine, \\
        "The hand that made us is divine."

\end{enumerate}

        To God our Saviour, who, from light above, \\
        Did in His Son a great Deliverer send, \\
        Who freed us by His work of mighty love.

        I'll strengthen thee, help thee, and cause thee to stand, \\
        Upheld by My righteous omnipotent hand.

    \item "When through the deep waters I call thee to go, \\
        The floods of distress shall not thee overflow; \\
        For I will be with thee, thy troubles to bless, \\
        And sanctify to thee thy deepest distress.

    \item "When through fiery trials thy pathway shall lie, \\
        My grace all-sufficient shall be thy supply; \\
        The flame shall not hurt thee; I only design \\
        Thy dross to consume and thy gold to refine.

    \item "E'en down to old age all My people shall prove \\
        My sovereign, eternal, unchangeable love; \\
        And when hoary hairs shall their temples adorn, \\
        Like lambs they shall still on My bosom be borne.

    \item "The soul that on Jesus hath leaned for repose, \\
        I will not, I cannot desert to its foes; \\
        That soul, though all hell should endeavour to shake, \\
        I'll never, no never, no never forsake."

\end{enumerate}

\begin{enumerate}

        His purposes lie there.

    \item The Lord thy God is with thee, \\
        He seeth every tear; \\
        Thy weight of grief He knoweth, \\
        Thou still to Him art dear. \\
        Thy prayers, e'en though unuttered, \\
        Pent in thine aching heart, \\
        The Lord thy God doth hear them, \\
        And with thee bears a part.

    \item The Lord thy God is with thee, \\
        Although thy way seem hard; \\
        In keeping His commandments \\
        Thine is a sure reward. \\
        Though called to suffer hardship \\
        In fighting for His name, \\
        The Lord thy God is with thee, \\
        His promise still the same.

    \item The Lord thy God is with thee, \\
        O saint, do not despair; \\
        His mighty arm is round thee; \\
        Confide in Him thy care. \\
        If e'er thou growest weary \\
        And feel'st how weak thy strength, \\
        Forget not He is with thee; \\
        Thou must o'ercome at length.

    \item The Lord thy God is with thee; \\
        Then bright shall be the dawn \\
        Of day that bringeth victory, \\
        The fruit of sorrows borne. \\
        And looking o'er thy pathway, \\
        With joy thou then wilt say, \\
        "The Lord my God was with me \\
        Each moment of the day."

\end{enumerate}

    \\
    \textit{\small [Moderately fast]}

\begin{enumerate}
    \item "ABBA, Father," Lord, we call Thee, \\
        Hallowed name from day to day. \\
        'Tis Thy children's right to know Thee, \\
        None but children "Abba" say. \\
        This high glory we inherit, \\
        Thy free gift through Jesus' blood; \\
        God the Spirit with our spirit \\
        Witnesseth we're sons of God.

    \item Though our nature's fall in Adam \\
        Shut us wholly out from God, \\
        Thus it was His counsel brought us \\
        Nearer still through Jesus' blood. \\
        For in Him we found redemption, \\
        Grace, and glory in the Son. \\
        Oh, the height and depth of mercy, \\
        Christ and we through grace are one!

    \item Richest stores of heavenly blessings \\
        God hath given through Christ, His Son; \\
        With the Holy Spirit's power \\
        Safe to lead His children on. \\
        Abba, Father, makes all certain, \\
        Both by word and oath and blood; \\
        Abba saith, "They are My people," \\
        And they say, "He is our God."

    \item Hence through all the changing seasons, \\
        Trouble, sorrow, sickness, woe, \\
        Nothing changeth God's affections, \\
        Abba's love shall bring us through. \\
        Soon shall all the blood-bought children \\
        Round the throne their anthems raise, \\
        And in songs of rich salvation \\
        Shout to Abba endless praise.

\end{enumerate}

        Trust thou in God, He'll keep thee to the end.
