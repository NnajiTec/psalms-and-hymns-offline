\begin{center}
    {\Large \textbf{HYMN 102}}
    \\
    \textit{8.8.8.8.8.8. MADRID}
    \\
    \textit{\small [Fast]}
\end{center}
\vspace{1em}

\begin{enumerate}
    \item THOU wondrous God, we love to trace \\
        The riches of Thy matchless grace; \\
        Thou, ere the worlds were made, didst plan \\
        To seek and save poor ruined man. \\
        We cannot praise Thee as we ought, \\
        Thy love excels our highest thought!

    \item Thou didst Thy Son in pity give, \\
        That sinners through His death might live; \\
        He suffered on the shameful tree \\
        To rescue us from misery. \\
        We cannot praise Him as we ought, \\
        His love excels our highest thought!

    \item The Holy Spirit, Teacher blest, \\
        Who guides us to our heavenly rest, \\
        Makes these poor hearts His dwelling place, \\
        To whisper there the tale of grace. \\
        We cannot praise Him as we ought, \\
        His love excels our highest thought!

    \item When Christ shall bring us in to Thee, \\
        We'll praise Thy grace more worthily; \\
        With all the blood-bought hosts above \\
        We'll magnify Thy boundless love; \\
        We fain would praise Thee as we ought, \\
        Thy love excels our highest thought!

\end{enumerate}

    \\
    \textit{C.M. EVAN}
    \\
    \textit{\small [Moderate]}
\end{center}
\vspace{1em}

\begin{enumerate}
    \item O GOD, how wide Thy glory shines, \\
        How high Thy wonders rise, \\
        Known through the earth by thousand signs, \\
        By thousands through the skies!

    \item Those mighty orbs proclaim Thy power, \\
        Their motions speak Thy skill; \\
        And on the wings of every hour \\
        We read Thy patience still.

    \item But when we view Thy great design \\
        To save rebellious worms, \\
        Our souls are filled with awe divine \\
        To see what God performs.

    \item For the bright glories of Christ's name \\
        Fill heaven and earth with praise; \\
        And angels learn Immanuel's fame, \\
        And celebrate His grace.

    \item Oh let us bear our humble part \\
        In that eternal song, \\
        Let joy and wonder tune each heart \\
        And love command each tongue.

\end{enumerate}

    \item Thou Sacred One, Almighty Three, \\
        Great everlasting mystery, \\
        What lofty numbers shall we frame, \\
        Equal to Thy tremendous name?

    \item Seraphs that stand around the throne \\
        Begin and speak the great Unknown; \\
        Attempt the song, wind up your strings \\
        To notes untried and boundless things.

    \item Ye whose capacious powers survey \\
        Largely beyond our eyes of clay, \\
        Yet what a narrow portion too \\
        Is seen, or known, or thought by you!

    \item How flat your highest praises fall \\
        Below th' immense Original! \\
        Weak creatures we that strive in vain \\
        To reach an uncreated strain!

    \item Great God, forgive our feeble lays, \\
        Sound out Thine own eternal praise; \\
        A song so vast, a theme so high, \\
        Calls for the voice that tuned the sky. Amen.

        Yes, now He lives no more to die. \\

    \item Through Him to God we now draw near, \\
        Of Him to God our praises bring. \\
        God for His sake will deign to hear, \\

        For all that Thou for us hast done \\
        In Christ Thy well-belov\`ed Son?

    \item When Israel's sons Thy presence sought, \\
        Acceptably to Thee they brought, \\
        As meet thank-offerings to be, \\

        For vict\`ories Thine arm achieved, \\

        Give, O Thou bless\`ed God, to Thee \\
        Than of the best that Thou hast given, \\
        Thy gift of gifts sent down from heaven?

    \item We make confession to His name, \\
        Who, loving and obedient, came \\
        To do Thy will, to meet our need, \\
        For Thee to live, for us to bleed.

    \item Though fails our understanding dull \\
        His worth to compass to the full, \\
        We yet would speak of Him to Thee; \\
        Dear to Thyself and us is He.

    \item We would adore Thee, God of love, \\
        Because of Him enthroned above, \\
        Through whom Thou hast Thyself made known, \\
        Thy purposes eternal shown.

    \item In His blest name our voice we raise \\
        In songs of gratitude and praise; \\
        Be Thou for evermore adored, \\
        Thou holy, wise, all-loving Lord.

    {\Large \textbf{HYMN 111}}
    \\
    \textit{L.M. MASON}
    \\
    \textit{\small [Moderate]}
\end{center}
\vspace{1em}

\begin{enumerate}
    \item SEE mercy, mercy from on high \\
        Descend to rebels doomed to die. \\
        'Tis mercy free which knows no bound, \\
        How grand, how gladsome is the sound!

    \item Soon as the reign of sin began \\
        The light of mercy dawned on man, \\
        When God announced the early news, \\
        ``The woman's Seed thy head shall bruise.''

    \item Brightly it beamed on man forlorn, \\
        When Christ, the holy Child, was born, \\
        And in its fullest splendour shone \\
        When Jesus, dying, cried ``'Tis done.''

    \item It triumphed when from death He rose \\
        And brake the power of all His foes; \\
        And since He took His seat on high \\
        Now mercy reigns eternally.

\end{enumerate}
